Questions & answers

Let's clear a few things up.

From the first guest conversation to a portfolio-wide view, here's what you need to know about working with Avarin.

Does the 14-day trial require a credit card?+

No. The 14-day free trial starts without payment details. You can test in-stay guest interaction and review response drafting immediately, with billing configured only if you decide to activate your subscription.

What happens when the in-stay agent cannot resolve a guest request?+

An unresolved concern automatically becomes an operational issue with guest evidence, urgency rating, department assignment, and event logging for on-duty hotel staff.

How does the agent avoid hallucinating or inventing hotel policies?+

Guest guidance is strictly grounded in your verified property knowledge base and checked against uploaded guides. If verified evidence is absent, the agent clarifies or safely hands off to human staff.

Can Avarin compare and benchmark multiple properties across our group?+

Yes. The Plus tier includes cross-property benchmarking, portfolio momentum analytics, role-based access, and group-wide intelligence queries through Sia.

What does competitor analysis include?+

Avarin tracks public rating shifts, review volumes, observed response speeds, sentiment breakdown, and recurring guest themes against specified competitors. Base tracks 1 competitor per hotel; Plus tracks 3.

Is Sia the guest-facing agent or a manager tool?+

Sia is your manager-facing agent, available through text and voice. Managers can investigate reviews and issues, compare data they can access, and request permitted actions. The separate in-stay agent communicates with guests over WhatsApp.

What can I do with Sia by voice?+

Ask about guest feedback, investigate open issues, compare data you can access, and request actions such as assigning an issue or updating its status. Calls show live captions and keep the spoken transcript in your selected Sia chat. Voice follows the same tools and permissions as text Sia.

How does automated review response work?+

Avarin connects directly to Google Business Profile to monitor new public reviews. The AI generates contextual replies matching your hotel's tone of voice, with options for fully automated publishing or approve-first workflows.

Which integrations are currently supported?+

Avarin currently integrates with WhatsApp for guest-facing in-stay messaging and Google Business Profile for review monitoring and automated responses.

How is guest and property data handled securely?+

All guest conversations and property knowledge documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. Data is isolated per hotel organization and is never used to train generalized external models.

How long does onboarding and setup take for a hotel?+

Setup typically takes under 48 hours. The team uploads existing property guides, connects WhatsApp and Google Business Profile, and verifies brand voice guidelines before going live.

Which languages does Avarin support for guest conversations and reviews?+

Avarin supports over 95 languages natively. The in-stay agent detects the guest's language over WhatsApp, and review responses are drafted in the exact language of each reviewer.

How does pricing scale as our hospitality group adds properties?+

Pricing is calculated per property per month. Volume discounts are available for portfolios with 5 or more properties, with centralized billing and organization-wide admin controls.
