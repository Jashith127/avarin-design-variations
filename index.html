For the GM or owner.

You can't read every review, chase every issue and still run the hotel. Sia does the reading, sorting and chasing, and brings you the few things that need a decision.

Today

Your day, already sorted.

Open one page each morning and see what happened, what changed and what needs you first. Reviews, negative reviews and your average rating sit beside a ranked list: Act now, Investigate, Monitor. You stop checking five sites to know where you stand.

  • What happened, what changed, what needs you
  • Ranked: Act now, Investigate, Monitor
  • Click any item to open the evidence behind it

Reviews and drivers

See what's costing you stars.

Sia ranks the parts of the guest experience holding your average rating down, with the mentions behind each one, so your attention goes to the thing that moves the score. It explains patterns in your reviews. It doesn't claim to prove cause.

  • Ranked by how much each one hurts your average
  • Mention counts, and the reviews behind each
  • A pattern explanation, not proof of cause

Competitors

Know where you stand, and why.

Track up to three nearby hotels. You get a plain headline: where you're ahead, where you're behind, and the biggest gap worth a training or recognition push. It uses public ratings and reviews.

  • Up to three tracked hotels
  • Ahead or behind by area: service, rooms, location, speed
  • Built from public ratings and reviews

Reports

The report you'd have built by hand.

Daily and monthly performance reports arrive by email or Telegram on the schedule you set, with an optional voice briefing. Every chart opens the reviews behind it, and you can share a read-only link with your owners.

  • Daily and monthly reports, on your schedule
  • Email or Telegram, with an optional voice briefing
  • A read-only link to share; every chart opens its reviews

For department heads.

Your department heads shouldn't need you to hand them problems. Each one reaches them with the guest's words, a suggested fix and a clear owner.

Issues

Problems arrive with proof.

Every issue lists the reviews, room QR notes and survey answers behind it, how urgent it is, and a suggested action to accept or edit. Feedback about the same pattern lands in one issue, not five messages.

  • Reviews, room QR notes and surveys attached
  • An urgency and a suggested action to edit
  • One issue per pattern, not five messages

Assignment

Every problem has an owner.

Issues are assigned to someone in the right department, or auto-assigned to its staff or manager. If a department has nobody yet, Sia says so, so a problem never sits with no one.

  • Assign to a person, or auto-assign
  • A warning when a department has nobody
  • Role and department shown on the issue

History

Nothing quietly goes cold.

Open, In Progress, Solved, with notes, reassignments and evidence all on the record. If the problem comes back, reopen it and see exactly what was done before.

  • Open, In Progress, Solved or Dismissed
  • Notes and reassignments on a timeline
  • Reopen it if the problem comes back

What is Sia?

Sia is your hotel team's assistant. She gathers reviews, QR feedback and surveys, drafts replies, and turns problems into issues for the right department. You stay in charge.

Which review sites does Sia read?

Google, Tripadvisor, Booking.com and Expedia, plus the room QR notes and surveys your guests send you.

Is Sia guest-facing?

No. Sia works with your managers, by text or voice. Guests may see a short note from a separate in-stay agent after QR feedback or a survey.

Who approves and posts replies?

Your team approves. You choose whether high ratings are approved automatically. A person always posts.

Does Sia hide guest feedback?

No. Unhappy guests can always tell you what happened, and happy guests see your review links. Sia never filters or posts reviews.

Which languages does it support?

Replies in 31 languages, with translations for your team. The workspace is available in English, Arabic, Indonesian and Malay.

Can Sia work across a group?

Yes. Plus adds group analytics, questions across properties, and roles for each person.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is per property. Get in touch and we'll help you choose the right plan.
