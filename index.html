Questions, answered.

Is Sia the guest-facing agent or a manager tool?

Sia is your manager-facing agent, available through text and voice. Managers can investigate reviews and issues, compare data they can access, and request permitted actions. The separate in-stay agent communicates with guests during their stay.

Who approves and posts review replies?

Replies are drafted for your team to edit and approve. You choose whether positive reviews are approved automatically. A person posts every reply.

Can Sia work across a hotel group?

Yes. Plus adds group analytics, cross-property questions and portfolio controls. Group leaders see only the portfolio they are allowed to access.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is per property. Contact sales for current pricing and help choosing the plan that fits your property or group.
